Answer Key

1know— stative verb, cannot be continuous
2works— habit + 3rd person singular = Simple
3usually go— frequency adverb + routine = Simple
4want— stative verb, no continuous form
5is recording— action in progress = Continuous (spelling error only)
6always check— adverb of frequency goes between subject + verb
7have— scheduled/stative "have" = Simple
8has been studying— duration from past to now = Present Perfect Continuous

5
Speaking Sprint
2 minutes · Record yourself · No stopping to translate
Speaking

"Describe your typical Monday — what do you usually do, and what's different about this Monday specifically?"

Use Present Simple for your regular routine
Use Present Continuous for what's different today
Use at least 3 of these words:
usually always right now at the moment currently never sometimes today
No pausing to translate. If you don't know a word, describe it differently and keep going.

Self-check after recording

  • Used Present Simple for habits and routine
  • Used Present Continuous for what's happening now
  • Used at least 3 time/frequency words from the list
  • Spoke for 2 full minutes without stopping
  • Did not pause to translate into Portuguese
